# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pledgr to be a place where founders, agent builders and backers can work together without fear of harassment or bad faith.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being direct and kind in reviews
- Assuming good intent, then verifying with evidence
- Accepting that a verdict, like a code review, can go against you
- Taking responsibility and fixing mistakes

Behaviour that does not:

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks of any kind
- Publishing others' private information
- Gaming the arbiter, forging attestations or colluding on appeals
- Anything a reasonable person would consider unprofessional

## Enforcement

Report problems to conduct@pledgr.si. Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and respond within five working days. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban from the project and the network.
